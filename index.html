THE ANSWER TO “WHAT ABOUT THE ENCODER?”
A video ViT builds the scene memory.
First, gather pixels.
A tubelet is a fixed image region spanning two consecutive frames. Its 2 × 16 × 16 RGB values give 1,536 numbers. A learned projection embeds them into C channels. Position information preserves where and when the token came from.
24 temporal groups × 16 × 16 spatial addresses = 6,144 video tokens. The original aspect ratio is supplied as a separate embedded token.
Then, exchange information.
The 40-layer ViT-g interleaves local frame-wise and global self-attention. Local attention mixes spatial evidence within a token-time group; global attention connects the entire clip, including different positions and later times.
The continuous feature bank F can carry information from far beyond each original tubelet. It is not an explicit voxel grid or a set of VQ code IDs.
Self-attentionQ = XW_Q · K = XW_K · V = XW_V
A = softmax(QKᵀ / √d_head + mask)
context = AV
All three streams come from video features. In the decoder, point queries supply Q and scene features supply K and V.

HOW THE WEIGHTS ARE LEARNED
One update, from clip to gradient.
01
Initialize
VideoMAE-pretrained encoder weights provide a starting point. D4RT's ablation shows an advantage over random initialization. Masked-video pretraining and geometric supervision are distinct objectives.
02
Sample
Augment a 48-frame clip consistently. Sample 2,048 queries from available trajectories; 30% target depth or motion boundaries. A 40% branch forces target time to equal camera time.
03
Predict
Encode once, decode queries independently. RGB and queries enter the model. Known trajectories and camera geometry construct labels outside the forward path.
04
Compare & update
Available labels activate losses. Gradients reach heads, decoder, query embeddings and encoder. AdamW changes weights; the next forward pass recomputes F.

Missing labels mask their losses. Occluded points can still have known 3D trajectories in synthetic data. Training mixes Kubric, PointOdyssey, Dynamic Replica, ScanNet and other public and internal sources.
The optimization recipe
500,000 AdamW steps; local batch 1 across 64 TPU chips; weight decay 0.03. Warm up for 2,500 steps to 10⁻⁴, then cosine decay to 10⁻⁶. Clip gradient L2 norm at 10. Reported duration: just over two days.

GENIE · ORIGINAL 2024 PAPER
Spatial → causal temporal
Each ST block mixes spatial locations, then time at each location with future positions masked, followed by a feed-forward layer. The video tokenizer quantizes representations into discrete codes.
RGB → ST tokenizer → VQ codes
past codes + action → next codes → RGB
A temporal sublayer alone follows one spatial address. After spatial mixing, that address carries information from other locations. Compare direct connections and this two-hop route in the encoder walkthrough.
